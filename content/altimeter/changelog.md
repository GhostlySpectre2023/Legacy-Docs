# Changelog

Version history of the Skydive Altimeter.

## 1.0.0

Released 2026-10-27.

### Added

- Analog altimeter NUI (x1000 ft, two-turn dial, danger zones, smooth needle)
- Audible alarm with configurable altitudes, number of beeps and volume
- AGL and MSL modes
- Job restriction: per job and minimum grade, job types and a duty requirement
- Server-side access check (job, grade, duty, item)
- Turns off automatically when the item is removed or the job changes
- Supports Qbox, QBCore and ESX, detected automatically
- Supports ox_inventory, qb-inventory and the ESX inventory
- 14 languages: en, nl, de, fr, es, pt, it, pl, tr, sv, da, cs, ro, ar
