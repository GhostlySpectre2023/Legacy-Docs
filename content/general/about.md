# About Legacy Scripts

Legacy Scripts is a FiveM development studio that builds premium, framework-ready resources, founded by GhostlySpectre.

## Founder

- **Name:** GhostlySpectre
- **Role:** Founder & Lead Developer
- **Company:** Legacy Scripts

**GhostlySpectre** founded Legacy Scripts and is its lead developer: they design, write and maintain the resources documented on this site, and these docs too.

## What we stand for

- **Premium quality.** Every resource is tested on a live server before it is released.
- **Works with your setup.** Qbox, QBCore and ESX support through open bridge files wherever possible.
- **Open where it matters.** Configs, locales and bridges stay readable after Cfx.re Asset Escrow, so you can adapt them to your server.
- **Real support.** Questions and bugs are handled by the people who wrote the code. See [Support](#/general/support).
