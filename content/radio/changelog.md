# Changelog

Version history of the Tactical Radio.

## 1.0.0

Initial release.

- Short range: PRC-152, PRC-148, patrol (police / EMS), PMR446, marine VHF, diver comms, scanner
- Long range: RT-1523 manpack and vehicle, mobile radio, PRC-160-style aviation / long-range panel
- Faceplates rebuilt after real-world reference photos (PRC-152, PMR446, patrol touchscreen, PRC-148, PRC-160)
- Nets with group permissions, locks, priorities, emergency override and collisions
- Encryption keys (gameplay access control): fill devices, generations / rotation, expiry, strength, secure repeaters
- Propagation: terrain volumes, interiors / underground, elevation, weather, interference zones, repeaters and networks
- Vehicle radios, crew intercom (PTT and VOX), installation kits
- Battery, charging, damage, water damage, repair; profiles and radio cloning
- Admin panel, coverage visualisation, signal test, metadata logs, audit trail, performance counters
- Editable hooks (`client/editable.lua`, `server/editable.lua`) and Cfx.re Asset Escrow layout
