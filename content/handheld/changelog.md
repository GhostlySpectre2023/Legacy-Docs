# Changelog

Version history of the TACNET tactical handheld and tablet.

## 1.0.0

Released 2026-10-27.

### Added

- Two devices: rugged Android-style handheld and rugged tablet with F-key row
- Compact and large size, switched with one key (`Config.ResizeKey`) or a hardware button
- Blue force tracking with fireteam colours, vehicle grouping and casualty status (OK / WIA / KIA)
- Friendly vehicle tracking
- Tactical map: MAP (dark) / DAY / TAC modes, 1 km / 100 m grid, 6-digit grid, distance and bearing
- 12 shared, time-stamped marker types with direction of travel; TIC and MEDEVAC alert the net
- Navigation to units, markers, routes and points (nav line and GPS waypoint)
- Shared route planning per fireteam
- Messaging: all stations and direct messages, unread badges, notifications
- Squad lead: assign fireteams, hand over lead, clear markers
- UAV feed (tablet): launch, retask, altitude, recall; slew, zoom, DAY / NV / thermal, mark target
- Helmet camera feed (tablet)
- Battery stored on the item, battery item to replace it
- Net, PIN, callsign and fireteam saved on the item
- Night mode and brightness
- Supports Qbox, QBCore and ESX (detected automatically) with ox_inventory
- 14 languages for the whole UI and all notifications: en, nl, de, fr, es, pt, it, pl, tr, sv, da, cs, ro, ar
